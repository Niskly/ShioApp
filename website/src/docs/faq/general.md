# General

Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Shio?

**Shio** is an Android app that lets you read manga, webtoons, and comics from a wide variety of sources — all in one place.

## Why is Shio not on the Google Play Store?

**Shio** is not available on the **Google Play Store**.

APK-based extensions conflict with Google Play content policies, so we distribute directly via GitHub releases.

## Is Shio available for iOS/iPadOS?

No. Shio is Android-only. Any app claiming to be "Shio for iOS" is not affiliated with us.

## Where do I download Shio?

Head to the [Download](/download/) page to get the latest APK directly from our GitHub releases.

## How do I install the APK?

1. Download the APK from the [Download](/download/) page.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. If prompted, allow installation from unknown sources in your Android settings.
4. Follow the on-screen steps to complete installation.

## What Android version does Shio require?

**Android 8.0 (Oreo)** or higher.

## How do I update Shio?

Download the latest APK from the [Download](/download/) page and install it over your existing version — your data will be preserved.
