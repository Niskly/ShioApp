## About

**Shio** is a free and open-source Android app for reading manga, webtoons, and comics, and watching anime — all from hundreds of sources in one place.

- Hundreds of manga, webtoon, comic, and anime sources
- Automatic tracking with MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, and more
- Multiple reading modes and deep customization
- Easy backup and restore so you never lose your progress
